# Business model

LDoc Studio is a developer and creator studio platform for documentation, design, and software workflows.
Strategy:
1. Freemium Distribution: Give LDoc Studio Community freely to developers, teams, and creators worldwide to achieve rapid market domination and organic viral adoption.
2. Monetization: High-value LDoc Studio Offline & Enterprise editions ([[offer-ladder]]) for companies requiring privacy, air-gapped local execution, and premium pro features.
Target audience: Developers, solo founders, tech teams, and agencies ([[icp]]). Revenue model: Freemium user base conversion to paid offline studio licenses.
